Introduction to 3D Gaussian Splatting

3D Gaussian Splatting is a rasterization technique that converts 3D point clouds into high-quality, photorealistic scenes by drawing multiple gaussians on the screen. The method involves steps such as Structure from Motion for estimating a point cloud, converting points to gaussians, training using Stochastic Gradient Descent, and differentiable Gaussian Rasterization for fast real-time rendering. This technique is gaining attention due to its potential applications in Embodied AI research and its ability to produce high-quality scenes, though it faces challenges such as high VRAM usage and compatibility issues with existing rendering pipelines.
