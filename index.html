In a study titled "Breaking the Chain," researchers evaluated the faithfulness of large language models (LLMs) to intermediate structures in schema-guided reasoning pipelines. Across multiple LLM models and benchmarks, the research found that while models are consistent with their own intermediate structures, they fail to update predictions after intervention, indicating that apparent faithfulness is fragile when the intermediate structure changes. The study suggests that delegating derivation of final decisions to external tools can mitigate this issue, though stronger prompting and preference optimization yield only limited improvements.
