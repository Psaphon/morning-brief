The Museum of the City of New York will open the Puffin Foundation Center for Social Activism in fall 2023, expanding its focus on civic engagement and social justice with new exhibitions, programs, and interactive elements. Funded by a $8 million donation from the Puffin Foundation, this initiative aims to highlight activism across New York City's history and support underrepresented voices, aligning with broader efforts in museums to address controversial topics while navigating political pressures. Key figures include Sarah Seidman, curator of social activism at MCNY, and Perry Rosenstein, founder of the Puffin Foundation.

Guggenheim to Screen Artistic Portrait of Soccer Legend Zinédine Zidane

The Guggenheim Museum will screen Douglas Gordon and Philippe Parreno’s film “Zidane, a 21st century portrait” from June 11 to July 19, coinciding with the FIFA World Cup. This 90-minute video captures a Real Madrid match entirely from Zidane's perspective, offering an intimate look at his technical mastery and emotional moments on the field. The screening is significant as it provides insight into both soccer and portraiture art, though its timing comes amid controversy surrounding FIFA’s ethics and governance.
