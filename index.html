London's sixth edition of Gallery Weekend, involving over 120 galleries, aims to promote art as relevant to everyone amid economic and political challenges facing the city. The event seeks to address the need for London to better engage with local philanthropists, policymakers, and publics who may not yet recognize the value of its vibrant gallery scene, which remains strong despite broader economic headwinds and a fractured global art market.

<i>I Ciclopi (Eye, Camera, Myth)</i> at INCURVA

I Ciclopi (Eye, Camera, Myth) is an exhibition featuring works by artists Dora Budor, Jana Euler, Jasper Marsalis, Simon Dybbroe Møller, Nina Porter, Margaret Raspé, and Davide Stucchi at INCURVA in Trapani from April 2 to June 28, 2026. Curated by Saim Demircan, the exhibition explores themes related to vision and technology through various media, including ceramics.
