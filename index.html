<i>Call me gravity</i> at WIELS

The exhibition "Call me gravity" is taking place at WIELS in Brussels from June 12 to September 20, 2026, featuring works by artists Dora Budor, Niloufar Emamifar, Annaik Lou Pitteloud, Georgia Sagri, Gianna Surangkanjanajai, and Sung Tieu. Curated by Pauline Hatzigeorgiou, the exhibition explores themes related to gravity through various ceramic and visual art pieces.
