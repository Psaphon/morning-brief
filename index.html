The War on Art Is Repeating Itself

Isaac Butler's book "The Perfect Moment" highlights the parallels between the Culture Wars of the 1980s and 1990s, where conservative forces targeted artists and their works, and the current challenges faced by the arts community, such as the postponement of exhibitions due to controversial content. Scientists have contested the widely accepted June 1889 date for Vincent van Gogh's "The Starry Night," suggesting the painting might depict a July night instead based on celestial alignments and other evidence.
