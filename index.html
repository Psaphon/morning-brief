A report from Critical Minded and the Dorothea and Leo Rabkin Foundation found that 45% of independent arts publications in the United States face an existential crisis due to declining funding, with a notable correlation between free-speech pressures and financial instability. The survey, which polled 141 independent outlets covering the arts, highlights how external pressures from advertisers and sponsors can impact the sustainability of these publications.
